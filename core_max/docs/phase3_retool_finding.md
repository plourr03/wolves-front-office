# The retool finding: keep Gobert, fix the Randle slot

The Phase-3 verdict said Gobert is too good to move for capital-realistic returns. This tests the
question that opens: keep Gobert (and the elite defense), keep Joan developing as a BACKUP (bust
risk contained to ~10 min, not the starting 5), re-sign Ayo, and swap ONLY Randle's bad-shooting
minutes for a real creator/shooter. Common random numbers isolate the Randle->creator swap.

## Result (Year-1 title probability, vs status quo)

| retool creator | status quo | retool | delta | 90% CI | P(retool>SQ) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MPJ (BKN, available; flat + stacks usage) | 2.42% | 2.69% | +0.26pp | [-1.70, +2.63] | 55% |
| Jrue Holiday (POR seller, age 35) | 2.42% | 3.05% | **+0.63pp** | [-1.15, +3.20] | 67% |
| Cam Johnson (DEN shooter, shaky seller) | 2.42% | 3.24% | +0.82pp | [-0.90, +3.38] | 74% |

## What it says

The Randle slot is the value; the Gobert slot is the cost. The two halves of Fork B decompose
cleanly:
- Randle -> creator: about +0.3 to +0.8pp (an upgrade).
- Gobert -> wing + Joan-starts: about -1.7 to -2pp (the hole that sank Fork B).
Doing only the good half (keep Gobert, retool Randle) RAISES near-term title odds; doing both
(Fork B) lowers them by ~1.1pp. The swing between the two strategies is ~1.4 to 2pp on a ~2.5%
base, large in relative terms.

This reframes the project's core thesis: the spec bet on moving the wrong big. The LAFI work
correctly diagnosed an OFFENSIVE-ARCHITECTURE problem (Randle's non-shooting minutes, the clog),
but the prescription (Fork B) moved Gobert too, the elite DEFENSE that is the team's real edge.
The data says: fix the offense by retooling the Randle slot with a SHOOTER (Cam-type best on
fit, since it un-clogs; Jrue the realistically-available impact upgrade), keep Gobert, and let
Joan develop behind him instead of handing him the keys at a ~-0.6 year-1 mean.

## Honest caveats

- Marginal, not transformational: +0.3 to +0.8pp keeps the Wolves a mid-tier ~2.5-3.2% team,
  not a contender. The CIs still span zero on the low end (the tilt is favorable, P 55-74%, the
  opposite of Fork B's 4-24%, but it is not a certainty).
- Seller realism: Cam Johnson (best fit, a shooter) is a shaky seller (Denver is a contender);
  Jrue (POR, +0.63) is the genuinely-available impact upgrade; MPJ (available, but flat impact
  and stacks on-ball usage) is only +0.26. The realistically-attainable retool is ~+0.3 to +0.6.
- CBA: a Cam retool ($23M < Randle $33.3M) opens room and is gate-clean; a Jrue ($34.8M) or MPJ
  ($40.8M) retool is tighter (take-back-more, first-apron hard cap) and must be run through the
  Phase-0 gate before it is treated as feasible.

## The larger question this raises

Even the best capital-realistic move leaves the Wolves at ~3%. That points at the deeper
question: is ANY available move material, or are the Wolves asset-constrained into the mid-tier
for this window? That is the next and most valuable thing to ask this engine.
