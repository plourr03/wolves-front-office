## 7. What Q0B v1 does NOT do

- **Detailed comp-set age curves.** The archetype curves are based on rough industry knowledge plus the Q7 historical comp trajectories. A formal comp-set age curve (50+ players per archetype, fitted curves with confidence bands) would tighten projections. Deferred.
- **Specific year-by-year RAPM projections.** The "production multipliers" are heuristic relative tiers. A formal multi-year RAPM forecast with bands would be tighter. Deferred.
- **Injury risk modeling.** Gobert, Randle, DiVincenzo all have non-trivial injury risk. A formal injury risk projection (probability of missing X games per year) would tighten contention score bands. Deferred.
- **League-wide trajectory comparison.** Where do other Western Conference contenders' windows align? OKC's window is timed similarly (young core). Boston has a different window. This comparative analysis is deferred.

These would sharpen Q0B v1 but the central window framing (2026-27 through 2028-29 peak, with summer 2026 and trade deadline 2027 as the active trade windows that enable 2027 cap relief) is robust.
