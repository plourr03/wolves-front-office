# LAFI clip: the 1:30 cut, beat by beat

Runs in parallel with **The LA Fitness Game**
(`postmortem/articles/01_lafi_article_first_draft.md`). One clip, one arc: the
Wolves played distributed pickup, and on the three components that actually
predict playoff offense they ranked third in the NBA. Every metric gets 8 to 10
seconds with a real hold, so it breathes instead of flashing.

Chart sources: the fingerprint
(`postmortem/outputs/charts/q0a_lafi/fingerprint_fragment.html`) and the Sharp
LAFI ranking (real values from
`postmortem/outputs/tables/q0a_lafi/lafi_composite_5component.csv`).
Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30fps, about 92 seconds.

## What makes it feel human (not templated)

- Your voice in the copy, verbatim.
- Real holds and stillness after each move, instead of constant motion.
- A film-room telestrator: a hand-drawn circle marks the number we're studying.
- An editorial chyron (left-aligned, chapter-numbered, a bucket-colored rule)
  instead of bouncing per-word kinetic text.
- Flat, article-style bars on a quiet backdrop, not the glossy glow look.
