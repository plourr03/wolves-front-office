## Executive summary

Three players, three honestly different cases:

**Donte DiVincenzo: KEEP, PROTECT THE RECOVERY, ADD CATEGORY B REDUNDANCY.** The cleanest empirical foundation in the project. All three methods (RAPM, raw on/off, lineup-grain) point positive. His 2025-26 RS catch-and-shoot production (6.05 attempts/game at 38.3% on 496 total attempts in 82 games) was elite Category B. The Achilles tear creates the largest structural gap on the roster. The decision is not whether to keep him (he's locked in by contract and injury); the decision is how aggressively to invest in his recovery and how to add Category B redundancy in case he doesn't return to form.

**Rudy Gobert: KEEP, WITH CONDITIONS.** RAPM #1 in our 565-player sample is the strongest single impact finding in the project. His defensive RAPM of -6.89 per 100 possessions is the largest defensive impact in the data. The Edwards+Gobert lineup-grain pairing was the team's best high-minute pairing in both 2024-25 RS (+6.07) and 2025-26 RS (+4.38). The case for trading him weakened substantially when the YoY lineup comparison showed Gobert+Randle was positive in both regular seasons (the playoff -13.26 finding was Spurs-specific, not structural). The condition: the team must invest in Category B around him. Gobert with Category B works. Gobert without Category B is more vulnerable to defensive schemes like the Spurs'.

**Julius Randle: USE CORRECTLY IN 2026-27, ACTIVE TRADE WINDOW THROUGH 2027.** Mixed empirical case. RAPM at -0.88 (slightly below replacement) but pairings with Edwards (+2.7 in 2025-26 RS) and Gobert (+4.6 in 2025-26 RS) positive. He functions adequately as a complementary piece but is not a foundation. The 2025-26 PO collapse (-16.30 on/off) is partly Spurs-specific 3PA suppression of the team plus standard small-sample exaggeration. The 2024-25 PO performance was meaningfully better. **REVISED contract framing:** his 2026-27 ($33.3M) is GUARANTEED; his 2027-28 ($35.8M) is the player option (he will almost certainly opt in given age 33 and likely market). The active trade windows are summer 2026, trade deadline 2027, or summer 2027. If no upgrade-trade lands, the default is "use correctly in 2026-27" (Edwards or Gobert pairings, avoid Edwards-OFF Randle-ON minutes).

The four-scenario combination analysis (Section 6) shows the data does not support a single dominant scenario. The strongest evidence-based recommendation is the **"Keep all three, prioritize Category B acquisition" path**, with the Randle disposition being more conditional than confident.

## 1. The DiVincenzo case (lead)

### 1.1 What the data says

**Triangulated positive across all three methods. The strongest single empirical foundation in the project.**

| Measure | DiVincenzo result | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **RAPM net** | +1.35 (rank #50 of 565) | Adjusted impact positive, moderately sized |
| **2025-26 RS on/off** | +10.52 net rating differential | DiVincenzo-ON: +5.40 over 2435 min; DiVincenzo-OFF: -5.12 over 1509 min |
| **2025-26 RS lineup-grain** | +7.57 net rating, **95% CI [+3.0, +12.2]** | Statistically positive over 2463 min |
| **2024-25 RS lineup-grain** | +4.08 (DiVincenzo-on), nearly equal to +4.22 (off) | Direction consistent but smaller magnitude |
| **2024-25 PO on the floor** | -10.66 (392 min) | Inverse direction; small playoff sample |

The 2024-25 PO direction is the one data point against the case. The wider context: the 2024-25 PO version of DiVincenzo had similar individual stats to 2025-26 RS but the team played different basketball around him (NAW was on the roster as a Category B alternative; the rotation was different).

### 1.2 Catch-and-shoot production: the LAFI Category B test

DiVincenzo's catch-and-shoot tracking across three seasons:

| Season | Games | Catch-and-shoot 3PA | CS 3PM | CS 3P% | CS 3PA/game |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2023-24 (NYK) | 81 | 590 | 240 | 40.7% | **7.28** |
| 2024-25 (MIN) | 62 | 311 | 127 | 40.8% | 5.02 |
| **2025-26 (MIN)** | **82** | **496** | **190** | **38.3%** | **6.05** |

The 2025-26 RS DiVincenzo was a 6+ catch-and-shoot 3PA per game shooter at 38.3% across a full 82-game season. This is elite Category B production (as defined in the LAFI v1 framework: 5+ catch-and-shoot 3PA per game at 38%+ accuracy is the cleanest single Category B specification).

Cross-reference: Naz Reid took 4.47 cs_3PA/game at 38.1% in 2025-26 (lower volume; also a big, not a wing). McDaniels took 2.22 at 45.1% (high accuracy, low volume). Conley took 2.00 at 38.9% (low volume, aging). **No other Wolves wing or guard combined DiVincenzo's volume with his accuracy.**

### 1.3 The Achilles recovery question

The injury occurred mid-Round-1 vs DEN. Without external comp-set research (deferred to v2), the recovery projection bands cited in the Q8 spec rely on the general medical literature:

- **Best case (top quartile of historical guard Achilles tears age 25-30):** Partial 2026-27 contribution at reduced athleticism (~40-60 games at 70-80% effectiveness), full return in 2027-28.
- **Median case:** Minimal 2026-27 contribution (~30-45 games at reduced effectiveness), partial 2027-28.
- **Worst case (bottom quartile):** No meaningful 2026-27 contribution, partial 2027-28 at substantially diminished form.

DiVincenzo will turn 29 in February 2026. The Achilles tear occurred in April 2026. Realistic Q1 2027 return targets put him at minimum 9 months post-surgery, more likely 12-15 months for meaningful basketball. A full RS return in 2026-27 is unlikely; a partial return at reduced athleticism is the median case.

**This is a non-decision in the sense that the team cannot trade or release DiVincenzo at meaningful value during recovery.** The relevant decisions are around how to plan the 2026-27 roster assuming reduced DiVincenzo availability, and around whether to bet on his 2027-28 return.

### 1.4 The Category B gap

If DiVincenzo provides ~30-50% of his 2024-25 form in 2026-27 (median case), the team's catch-and-shoot 3PA volume from the wing position drops substantially. Internal options:

- **Naz Reid:** Could play more minutes but he's a big, not a wing. Can't fully replace DiVincenzo's role.
- **McDaniels:** Could expand volume from 2.22 to maybe 3.5+ cs_3PA/game if the offense designs more catch-and-shoot for him. His 45.1% accuracy supports the expansion.
- **Conley:** Aging (38 in 2025-26). Volume and accuracy both declining. Can't be a primary Category B option.
- **Edwards:** 49.6% on 139 cs_3PA in 2025-26 RS is excellent. But his volume is constrained by his role as primary creator.

**The gap is roughly 2-4 catch-and-shoot 3PA per game of high-volume elite-shooting wing production.** Closeable partially through expanded McDaniels role; partially through external acquisition.

### 1.5 The Q8 verdict on DiVincenzo

| Question | Probability |
|---|---|
| DiVincenzo contributes meaningfully in 2026-27 | 25-40% |
| DiVincenzo returns to 2024-25 form by 2027-28 | 35-55% |
| The Category B gap in 2026-27 is large enough to require external acquisition | 75-85% |

**Recommendation framing for Q5 Path 2 (Category B acquisition):** Path 2 is no longer one of three optional paths. It's a requirement for 2026-27. The DiVincenzo absence creates a structural Category B gap that internal expansion (McDaniels primarily) can partially close but not fully. External acquisition of a 3-4 cs_3PA/game wing at 37%+ accuracy is the cleanest path.

## 2. The Gobert case

### 2.1 What the data says

**Pre-committed hypothesis H1a:** Gobert's positional constraints suppress off-ball motion when he's on the floor. **Status:** Not directly tested in v1 (would require LAFI C2 motion tracking with Gobert on vs off; deferred). Suggestive evidence is mixed.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H1b:** Gobert remains a top-tier defensive impact player despite his age. **Status:** Strongly supported. RAPM def rating -6.89 is the lowest (best) in the 565-player sample.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H1c:** Gobert's fit is structurally worse with the current personnel than it was with KAT in 2023-24. **Status:** Mixed. The Edwards+Gobert pairing was +6.07 in 2023-24 RS, +4.38 in 2025-26 RS (small decline but still positive). The KAT-Randle swap may have created some fit deterioration, but Gobert remains a positive contributor to lineups with Edwards.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H1d:** The Wolves' offense improves measurably when Naz plays the 5 instead of Gobert. **Status:** Partially supported with caveat. Naz-at-5 lineups generate more 3PA (37.4 fg3a/100 PO vs 29.6 for Gobert-at-5 PO; +2.9 fg3a/100 RS difference). But net rating differences are smaller and not statistically established (CIs cross zero on most comparisons).

**Pre-committed hypothesis H1e:** Gobert's offensive limitations specifically cause the Wolves' Q4 motion-death pathology. **Status:** Not strongly supported by the data. The Edwards+Gobert RS pairing was the team's best high-minute pairing in both years. The motion-death pathology is real per LAFI v1 but is more plausibly attributed to the team-wide system shift (KAT departure, less designed off-ball motion) than to Gobert specifically.

### 2.2 The case FOR Gobert (the evidence that surprised me)

| Measure | Result | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **RAPM net (3-year pooled)** | **+6.18** | **#1 in 565-player sample**. Wembanyama is #2 at +4.90 |
| **RAPM def** | **-6.89** | Lowest (best) defensive impact in the sample |
| **Edwards+Gobert RS pairing** | +4.38 (2025-26), +6.07 (2024-25) | Team's best high-minute Edwards pairing in BOTH years |
| **Gobert+Naz RS pairing** | +5.78 (2025-26), +10.76 (2024-25, CI excludes 0) | Statistically positive in 2024-25 |
| **Gobert+Randle RS pairing** | +4.57 (2025-26), +5.71 (2024-25) | Positive in both years over 1200+ min each |
| **Raw on/off 2025-26 RS** | +5.26 | Top-2 differential on the team |

The picture: Gobert is consistently positive in regular-season contexts. The lineup-grain evidence and the RAPM converge.

### 2.3 The case AGAINST Gobert (the playoff data and the LAFI implication)

| Measure | Result | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Gobert+Randle 2025-26 PO** | -13.26 over 204 min, CI [-27.9, +1.5] | Robustly negative in 2025-26 PO vs SAS |
| **Gobert-at-5 2025-26 PO** | -9.88 over 211 min | Single-big alignment underperformed |
| **3PA rate (Gobert-at-5 PO)** | 0.327 vs 0.401 for Naz-at-5 | Gobert-at-5 generates fewer 3PA volume |
| **LAFI Q4 pathology** | Wolves are 90th percentile iso reliance, 83rd percentile shot quality decay | The team's offense has structural issues; Gobert is part of the architecture |
| **Age** | Will turn 34 in June 2026 | Defensive-anchor centers can decline rapidly at this age |

The 2025-26 PO data shows Gobert lineups underperformed. The yoy comparison reveals this was Spurs-specific (R2 -26.46 vs R1 -3.04 for Gobert+Randle), but the architectural vulnerability is real: against a Wembanyama-anchored defensive scheme, the team's offense with Gobert in struggled.

The age question is unanswered in v1. Gobert's RAPM is 3-year pooled. If he's declining (typical for defensive-anchor centers in their early 30s), his 2026-27 impact may be lower than his pooled estimate suggests. Weighted-recent RAPM comparison was flagged but not built in v1.

### 2.4 The honest reconciliation

The Q5 Reconciliation A vs B question: does Gobert's defensive value justify the LAFI Q4 offensive limitations, or does the LAFI framework overstate Gobert's offensive cost?

**Reconciliation A is supported by the data.** Gobert's defensive RAPM (-6.89) is large enough that even with some offensive cost (off RAPM -0.71, slightly negative), his net impact is the highest in the sample. The LAFI framework describes real offensive limitations (Q4 pathology, shot quality decay), but Gobert's defensive value outweighs his offensive cost in net rating terms.

**Important nuance:** The LAFI framework was never about Gobert specifically; it was about the team's offensive architecture. Gobert is a piece of that architecture, but the Q4 pathology emerged from the KAT-to-Randle swap (which removed offensive gravity), the DiVincenzo absence in R2 (which stripped Category B), and the team-wide structural shift in 3PA rate (which we now know is system-level, not Edwards-individual per the yoy comparison).

### 2.5 The Q8 verdict on Gobert

| Question | Probability |
|---|---|
| Gobert is a net negative on the current roster | **15-25%** |
| Gobert would be a net positive on this roster with Category B fully in place | **70-85%** |
| The realistic trade return for Gobert improves the team's championship odds | **20-30%** |

**Recommendation framing:** KEEP, WITH CONDITIONS. The empirical case for Gobert is strong. The trade case for moving him is now weak. But the conditional language matters: Gobert with Category B around him is a high-leverage piece. Gobert alone (with non-shooting bigs and limited wing shooting) is the version that struggles against teams like the Spurs.

**The case for keeping Gobert depends on whether the team can ensure Category B is in place around him.** This makes the DiVincenzo recovery and the Q5 Path 2 acquisition both more important: they're not independent decisions from Gobert. They're the precondition for Gobert's value to fully materialize.

## 3. The Randle case

Randle's iso PPP improved from 0.785 (2024-25 RS) to 0.943 (2025-26 RS). Frequency rose from 13.4% to 16.6%. Not directly compared to alternative iso options (Edwards iso PPP 1.036 in 2025-26 RS, higher than Randle's). Some allocation inefficiency visible but not extreme.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H2b:** Randle's regular season production declined from 2024-25 to 2025-26. **Status:** Not strongly supported. TS% basically identical (0.593 → 0.585). 3PA rate dropped (0.334 → 0.287). Iso PPP improved. Plus-minus per 36 improved (+2.73 → +3.25). Modest stat-line drift but not a clear decline.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H2c:** Randle's playoff production is systematically below his regular season production. **Status:** Supported, but the 2024-25 playoff version was a positive contributor to a conference finals run. The 2025-26 playoff version was much worse, but a substantial portion is Spurs-specific (per the yoy comparison).

**Pre-committed hypothesis H2d:** Randle's fit with Edwards is worse than alternative power forwards. **Status:** Hard to test without specifying the alternative. Edwards+Randle was +3.44 in 2024-25 RS and +2.68 in 2025-26 RS. Mediocre but positive.

**Pre-committed hypothesis H2e:** Randle's value is concentrated in specific game contexts. **Status:** The Edwards×Randle 2x2 (Section 3.3 below) tests this directly.

### 3.4 The Edwards×Randle 2x2 test (the key Q8 Randle hypothesis test)

The Q8 spec's H2e hypothesis: "the team's net rating is highest in 'Edwards on, Randle off' and lowest in 'Edwards off, Randle on.'" If supported, Randle's value to the team is conditional on Edwards being absent.

**2025-26 Regular Season (gt-filtered):**

| Cohort | Stints | Min | Net | 95% CI | 3PA rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Edwards ON, Randle ON | 675 | 1610 | +2.68 | [-2.5, +7.9] | 0.416 |
| Edwards ON, Randle OFF | 348 | 548 | -0.74 | [-10.0, +9.2] | 0.428 |
| Edwards OFF, Randle ON | 494 | 990 | +4.39 | [-2.8, +11.4] | 0.431 |
| Edwards OFF, Randle OFF | 395 | 785 | +4.54 | [-3.3, +12.8] | 0.408 |

The hypothesis is **not supported.** Edwards-OFF cohorts are slightly better than Edwards-ON cohorts (the bench-rest confound applies to both Randle-on and Randle-off versions). Randle's marginal value when added to Edwards-OFF lineups is essentially zero (Edwards-OFF Randle-ON +4.39 vs Edwards-OFF Randle-OFF +4.54). Randle's marginal value when added to Edwards-ON lineups is positive (Edwards-ON Randle-ON +2.68 vs Edwards-ON Randle-OFF -0.74, but CIs heavily overlap).

**2025-26 Playoffs:**

| Cohort | Stints | Min | Net | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Edwards ON, Randle ON | 116 | 238 | **-11.02** | [-24.5, +2.5] |
| Edwards ON, Randle OFF | 49 | 86 | +6.68 | [-18.6, +34.1] |
| Edwards OFF, Randle ON | 76 | 162 | +1.53 | [-14.0, +18.4] |
| Edwards OFF, Randle OFF | 48 | 86 | +13.93 | [-4.5, +31.2] |

The playoff data shows the Edwards+Randle pairing was catastrophic (-11.02 over 238 min, CI [-24.5, +2.5]) while Edwards+Randle-off was modestly positive. This is the Spurs-specific scheme effect amplified by small sample. The Edwards-OFF Randle-OFF cohort being +13.93 is essentially a bench-mob garbage-time effect (86 min in playoffs is small).

**2024-25 Regular Season (for comparison):**

2024-25 RS Edwards-OFF Randle-OFF was +10.31 with CI [+0.1, +19.4] (just barely excludes zero). The same pattern: Edwards-OFF lineups generally outperformed Edwards-ON lineups due to bench-rest. Randle's marginal value was essentially neutral across cohorts.

### 3.5 The synthesis: Randle is a complementary piece

The Edwards×Randle 2x2 doesn't show Randle as a clear net-negative. It also doesn't show him as a clear net-positive. He's a complementary piece whose marginal value is approximately neutral when added to lineups, with the bench-rest confound dominating cohort comparisons.

**For Q8, the cleanest framing is:**
- Randle's individual impact is approximately replacement-level
- His pairings with Edwards (+2.68) and Gobert (+4.57) are positive but modest
- His 2025-26 PO collapse was disproportionately Spurs-specific
- His 2024-25 PO was meaningfully better

The case for moving Randle is not the "trade him to upgrade" case strongly suggested. It's more the "swap him for a better-fitting archetype if available" case. If a stretch-4 catch-and-shoot specialist with playmaking is available at his salary slot, that's an upgrade. If the realistic alternative is a less-talented PF or a cap dump, the math may not improve.

### 3.6 The player option dimension [REVISED 2026-05-18]

**Previous framing was wrong about the option year.** Randle's 2026-27 ($33.3M) is GUARANTEED, not a player option. His 2027-28 ($35.8M) is his player option. The team has him locked in for 2026-27 regardless. The opt-in/opt-out decision happens in summer 2027 for the 2027-28 season.

The corrected scenarios for Randle:

**Locked in for 2026-27.** Randle is on the team at $33.3M for the 2026-27 season. The only ways to remove him are trade (summer 2026, trade deadline 2027, or summer 2027) or extension/restructure. The "use him correctly" path is the default action for 2026-27 if no trade lands. Deploy him mostly with Edwards or Gobert (positive pairings). Manage Edwards-OFF Randle-ON minutes carefully.

**Summer 2027 opt-in (most likely).** Randle exercises his $35.8M player option. He stays on the team. The team's decision becomes whether to trade him in summer 2027 with $35.8M as the matching salary, or hold through 2027-28 (his age 33 season, projected declining production).

**Summer 2027 opt-out (low probability).** Randle would only opt out if he believed he could secure a longer-term, higher-AAV deal in free agency. At age 33 with declining playoff production, this is unlikely. Most likely Randle opts in to guaranteed $35.8M rather than testing a market that may offer less.

**The trade decision is summer 2026, trade deadline 2027, or summer 2027 (with $35.8M option year typically included in the package the buyer wants).** The trade market for high-usage non-shooting 4s at his salary tier is limited; the realistic trade return may not be a clear upgrade. The "use him correctly" path becomes the default if no trade lands.

### 3.7 The Q8 verdict on Randle

| Question | Probability |

The probabilities are intentionally close to 50/50. The data does not strongly favor either keeping or moving Randle. The decision is more contingent on what's actually available in the trade/free-agent market than on a clean data finding.

**Recommendation framing (REVISED):** USE CORRECTLY IN 2026-27. Randle is locked in for 2026-27 ($33.3M guaranteed). Deploy him primarily with Edwards or Gobert (or both) and avoid the Edwards-OFF Randle-ON minutes that don't outperform Edwards-OFF Randle-OFF. The active trade decision is summer 2026, trade deadline 2027, or summer 2027 with his $35.8M option (which he will almost certainly opt in to). Trading him is more defensible from RAPM/salary-efficiency than trading Gobert (Q8 salary addendum: Randle has -3.38 surplus), but the matching salary for an upgrade requires combining his contract with other outflows.

## 4. Edwards as context (not a decision)

Edwards is untouchable. But the data tells us specific things about how to read the Q8 decisions in his context.

**Edwards offensive RAPM is +3.97.** Among the high-impact offensive players in the sample. Higher than SGA (+1.31), Doncic (+1.68), Jokic (+1.84) in this sample (with the caveat that Wolves-only data makes opponent benchmarks noisy). His offensive impact is established.

**Edwards' RAPM net of -0.61 is an artifact.** The Wolves-only sample can't separate his individual impact from "the Wolves' overall offense when he plays." Do not use this number in Q8 framing.

**Edwards' 3PA pullback is system-driven.** The yoy comparison showed Edwards-ON and Edwards-OFF lineups had nearly identical 3PA rates in both 2024-25 RS and 2025-26 RS. The team-wide YoY 3PA decline (-3.6 percentage points) is not Edwards' individual shot selection. It's a system/personnel change.

**Edwards' 2025-26 PO 3PA rate dropped to 0.276 in his normal-load games (R2 G3-G6).** The Spurs forced him off the line specifically. Q3 mechanism analysis (not built yet) should investigate why.

**Implication for Q8:** Edwards' development is independent of the Gobert/Randle/DiVincenzo decisions. He's the franchise. The question is whether the team builds the right complementary structure around him. The Q8 prescriptions about the other three players are about that structure.

## 5. The four-scenario combination analysis

### 5.1 Scenario definitions

Four roster combinations over Gobert and Randle (DiVincenzo is non-decision):

| Scenario | Gobert | Randle | DiVincenzo |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. Keep all three | KEEP | KEEP | RECOVERY (median case) |
| B. Trade Gobert, keep Randle | TRADE | KEEP | RECOVERY (median case) |
| C. Keep Gobert, trade Randle | KEEP | TRADE | RECOVERY (median case) |
| D. Trade both | TRADE | TRADE | RECOVERY (median case) |

For each scenario, we estimate the 2026-27 net rating implications and championship probability given the project's evidence. These are model outputs, not predictions. Wide ranges.

### 5.2 Scenario projections (qualitative)

**Scenario A: Keep all three.** Most consistent with the project's evidence. The team's regular-season identity is preserved. Gobert's RAPM-supported defensive value remains. The Gobert+Randle pairing's RS-positive history continues. Randle's complementary role with Edwards continues. The main risk is the Spurs-specific vulnerability: if the team faces SAS in the playoffs again, the 3PA collapse pattern likely repeats. The DiVincenzo recovery is the key variable; with healthy DiVincenzo, the team's ceiling is the 2024-25 WCF version. Without him, the Category B gap remains.

- Projected 2026-27 RS net rating: +3 to +5 (similar to 2025-26 RS +3.0 with potential upside from DiVincenzo partial return and McDaniels expanded role)
- Championship probability: 4-8% (similar to recent years, depends on Western Conference upper-end)
- Asset cost: zero
- Cap implication: stays in luxury tax range

**Scenario B: Trade Gobert, keep Randle.** Goes against the project's strongest empirical finding (Gobert RAPM #1). Defensive identity craters; Naz becomes the starting 5 (workable defensively but not Gobert-tier). Offensive identity opens (Naz adds Category B from the 5 position). Trade return likely doesn't include Gobert-tier defensive replacement. Risk: defensive rating drops by 4-8 points; offense improves by 2-4 points; net effect modestly negative.

- Projected 2026-27 RS net rating: -1 to +2 (decline from current)
- Championship probability: 2-5%
- Asset cost: depends on trade return; realistic centers available for max contracts are limited
- Cap implication: depends on incoming salary

**Scenario C: Keep Gobert, trade Randle.** The "use Randle's salary slot for upgrade" path. Edwards+Gobert pairing preserved. The Randle replacement matters: if it's a stretch-4 catch-and-shoot specialist, the LAFI Category B gap closes meaningfully. If it's a cap dump or a less-talented PF, the team gets worse. The Edwards+(stretch-4)+Gobert lineup is the LAFI-optimal configuration in theory but unproven in practice.

- Projected 2026-27 RS net rating: +3 to +6 (if Randle replacement is a clear upgrade; otherwise flat or worse)
- Championship probability: 5-10% (if upgrade lands)
- Asset cost: depends on what the team gives up to get the upgrade
- Cap implication: depends on incoming contract

**Scenario D: Trade both.** Most aggressive. Significant cap relief. Edwards-centered rebuild. Defensive identity craters AND offensive complementary piece is lost. Multiple bets on replacements have to work. Upside if hits, downside if misses.

- Projected 2026-27 RS net rating: -2 to +4 (very wide range)
- Championship probability: 2-8% (depends on replacements)
- Asset cost: significant; multiple moves required
- Cap implication: substantial cap space, but trade-down likely returns mid-tier salaries

### 5.3 Honest scenario ranking

The data supports Scenario A or C with C as the higher-upside / higher-execution-risk option.

**Scenario A is the data-conservative path.** Lowest execution risk. Preserves the empirical anchors (Gobert defense, Edwards+Gobert pairing, Gobert+Randle RS-positive history). The ceiling is the 2024-25 WCF version. Bets on DiVincenzo recovery and on the Spurs not being a recurring opponent.

**Scenario C is the data-aggressive path.** Higher upside if execution lands (Randle replacement is a clear LAFI-Category-B-friendly upgrade). Higher risk if execution misses. The Q8 evidence on Randle being approximately replacement-level (RAPM -0.88) makes the trade case not damning but also not foundational. The case for C depends on what's actually available in the market.

**Scenarios B and D are NOT supported by the empirical evidence in the project.** Trading Gobert forfeits the team's largest individual impact. The arguments for trading him rest on playoff-sample data that has been contextualized as opponent-specific.
