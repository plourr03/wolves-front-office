## What the deliverable previously assumed (incorrectly)

Q5 v3 and Q8 v1 framed the 2027-28 year as having team-controlled optionality on Gobert and Randle. The implicit logic was: "if their decline accelerates, the team can decline to exercise the 2027-28 non-guaranteed year and free up cap space." This logic does not exist. The team has no such option. The players hold those decisions.

Specific affected claims:

1. **Q5 v3 executive summary line 33:** "Preserve the 2027 cap reset (Gobert and Randle 2027-28 non-guaranteed years) as the strategic asset." The non-guaranteed-year framing is wrong; both are player options.

2. **Q5 v3 line 187:** "NOT trading Gobert. Positive RAPM, intact defense, non-guaranteed 2027-28 gives free optionality." The free optionality does not exist. Gobert controls the option.

3. **Q5 v3 Section 6 (the 2027 reset):** "If Gobert and Randle 2027-28 non-guaranteed years are not exercised, the team has approximately $80M cap room in summer 2027." Wrong premise. The cap room only opens via trades (or the unlikely scenario of either player declining a guaranteed paycheck).

4. **Q5 v3 line 269:** "Randle has a player option for 2026-27 at $33.3M. His 2027-28 ($35.8M) is non-guaranteed." Both halves are wrong. 2026-27 is guaranteed; 2027-28 is the player option.

5. **Q8 v1 Section 3.6:** "Randle has a player option for 2026-27." Wrong year for the option.

6. **Q8 salary addendum Section 2 and 3:** Multiple references to Randle 2026-27 as a player option and Gobert/Randle 2027-28 as team-controlled non-guaranteed. Both are wrong.

7. **Q8 weighted recent RAPM line 101:** "The 2027-28 non-guaranteed year gives the team optionality." The optionality is the player's.

8. **Q0B v1 findings (multiple lines):** "Gobert non-guaranteed 2027-28 year is well-timed: the team can re-evaluate." The framing implied team control. The decision is the player's.

## The corrected strategic landscape

**Summer 2027 is an active trade window, not a passive reset.** In summer 2027 the Wolves face:

- Gobert opts in to $38M (near-certain). Team can pay or trade.
- Randle opts in to $35.8M (near-certain). Team can pay or trade.
- DiVincenzo enters free agency. Team can bid against market.
- Conley likely retired.
- Beringer/Shannon team options likely exercised.

The "$80M cap room" framing in Q5 v3 only materializes if the team executes trades on both Gobert and Randle. Without trades the team is over the cap with declining production from its two highest-paid non-Edwards players.

### Four scenarios for the 2027-28 cap picture

**Scenario 1: No trades. Both opt in.** Gobert at $38M, Randle at $35.8M. Combined $73.8M for two declining players. DiVincenzo re-sign constrained (MLE or via TPE). Secondary creator acquisition via cap room is not available; would require another trade. Most likely default outcome if no proactive moves.

**Scenario 2: Trade Gobert before 2027-28 starts.** Cleanest timing: summer 2026 or trade deadline 2027. Opens $38M in cap. Asset cost depends on return (could be expiring contracts, picks, or a young player). Defensive impact lost; needs replacement. Randle stays at $35.8M.

**Scenario 3: Trade Randle before 2027-28 starts.** Opens $35.8M in cap. Asset cost depends on return. Per Q8 salary addendum, Randle is the lowest-surplus-RAPM contract on the roster, so trading him is more defensible than trading Gobert. Gobert stays at $38M.

**Scenario 4: Trade both before 2027-28 starts.** Opens combined $73.8M. The full theoretical reset. Requires two separate trades, each with asset costs. Highest cap-positive outcome but highest execution risk.

**Scenario 5: Earlier trades.** Either or both could be traded in summer 2026 or at the trade deadline 2027. Different return dynamics depending on contender vs rebuilder buyer.

Each scenario produces different prescription implications. The deliverable should engage with this multi-scenario framing.

## What changes in the prescription

### Portfolio C: "Preserve 2027 flexibility"

**Previous framing (Q5 v3):** Patient strategy that preserves automatic cap flexibility in summer 2027 for a major acquisition.

**Corrected framing:** Patient strategy contingent on trade execution. To access the cap flexibility that enables a secondary creator acquisition, the team must trade Gobert and/or Randle. The "patience" is not waiting for the cap to open automatically. It is preserving asset capital so that the necessary trades can return useful players or picks rather than salary dumps.

Portfolio C is still valid and still pairs with Portfolio A as a complementary multi-year plan. But the execution bar is higher than the previous framing implied.

### Q8 Gobert keep recommendation

**Previous framing:** Keep Gobert. Positive RAPM, intact defense, 2027-28 non-guaranteed gives free optionality.

**Corrected framing:** Keep Gobert for 2025-26 and 2026-27. The optionality argument is gone. Gobert holds the 2027-28 option and will exercise it. The team's options for removing him from the 2027-28 books are: trade him (in 2026-27 or summer 2027), accept paying $38M to a 35-year-old declining defensive anchor, or trade him at the 2028 trade deadline mid-season.

The cleanest trade window is summer 2026 or trade deadline 2027 (before his 2027-28 option exercises). If the team wants to extract trade value, the window for that is finite.

The recommendation to keep Gobert through 2026-27 still holds because (a) his RAPM is genuinely strong, (b) his defensive value during Edwards' rising-peak year is real, and (c) the system restoration described in Q5 v3 Section 5 is more valuable than what a trade return would deliver. But "keep through 2026-27" is different from "keep with free optionality for 2027-28." The 2027-28 path forward is active, not passive.

### DiVincenzo summer 2027 re-sign

**Previous framing:** Re-sign DiVincenzo aggressively in 2027 if he proves out.

**Corrected framing:** The ability to re-sign aggressively depends on the Gobert/Randle disposition. If Gobert and Randle both opt in (Scenario 1) and the team does not trade them, the Wolves are over the cap and can only re-sign DiVincenzo via the MLE or a non-Bird scenario. If the team executes trades on one or both, the cap room becomes available. The re-sign aggressiveness scales with the trade execution.

### Section 6 of Q5 v3

Replace "The 2027 reset (the strategic asset)" framing with "The 2027 active trade window." The reset is contingent. The team has options, but they are trade options, not waiver options.

### Contract decision points calendar (Q0B + Q5 v4 appendix)

**Previous framing (Q0B v1):** Summer 2027 has Gobert non-guaranteed decision, Randle non-guaranteed decision, DiVincenzo FA, Shannon expiring.

**Corrected framing:** Summer 2027 has Gobert opt-in (player decides, near-certain opt in), Randle opt-in (player decides, near-certain opt in), DiVincenzo FA, Beringer team option (team controls, near-certain exercise), Shannon team option. The team's active decisions are: whether to trade Gobert before his option exercises (summer 2026 to summer 2027), whether to trade Randle, DiVincenzo re-sign bid, and the team options.

The 2026-27 trade deadline (February 2027) becomes an important active decision point because it is the last chance to trade Gobert or Randle before their 2027-28 options lock in their salaries.

## The pattern observation

This is the second contract-structure error caught in this project. Both errors assumed the team had more control over outgoing contracts than the actual structure provides. The triangulation principle that has caught other errors throughout the project (DiVincenzo confound, Gobert+Naz lineup mislabel, Gobert+Randle Spurs-specific framing, Edwards injury context) applies here too. Contract-related claims should be confirmed against the actual structure (Spotrac standard) rather than inferred from raw salary CSV numbers. The Guaranteed total column is the verification: when it equals the sum of the first N years, only those N years are guaranteed; the remaining years are option years or non-guaranteed years and the distinction matters enormously.
