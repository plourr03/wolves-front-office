Second, variance: an offense this dependent on three-point volume can go cold in a playoff series, and when the threes stop falling there is no bail-out post hub or free-throw-generating big to keep the offense afloat. Layer in injury risk to Tatum's surgically repaired Achilles or to Brown, and a team built on two skilled, switchable wings can be reduced to a one-creator outfit that opponents load up against.
