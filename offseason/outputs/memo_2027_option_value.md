# Strategic Memo: The 2027 Option Value of the Bridge

_No new model build. This argues, using the break-even-availability framework already in
Component G, why a bridge year that stays competitive while spending no picks and keeping
2027 clean is worth more than a 2026 desperation-star swing._

## The bridge is a call option on information

The 2026 board's uncomfortable truth: the highest-ceiling targets are exactly the ones whose
verdict the model cannot resolve, because it turns on a health fact that is not yet known.
G prices that as the break-even availability a*, the suit-up rate at which the risk-adjusted
EV crosses zero:

| target | break-even a* | known health entering 2026-27 | verdict today |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kyrie | 43% | unknown (Year-1 ACL, no 2025-26 sample) | negative at the biased-low 32% base |
| AD | 83% | 49% and trending down | negative |
| Tatum (BOS) | n/a (opponent) | unknown (Year-1 Achilles) | the BOS variant swings 10-20% on it |
| Haliburton (IND) | n/a (opponent) | unknown (Achilles) | reshapes the East field |

Each of these is a coin the league has not yet flipped. By **July 2027** every one of those
coins has landed: Kyrie, AD, Tatum, and Haliburton will have a full 2026-27 observed
availability and on-court level. The bridge year does not waste 2027, it **buys the answer**.

## What the resolution is worth, in the framework's own units

The break-even column quantifies the swing directly. A fragile target observed healthy moves
from "gamble below break-even" to "clears break-even," and the EV swings by roughly the
distance between the two branches:

- **Kyrie:** at the biased-low 32% base he is -0.35pp and red-capped. Observed healthy at a
  credible Year-2 60%, he is **+0.55pp** and his metric-robust +2.27 raw becomes ownable. A
  swing of order **+0.9pp** of title equity, plus the elimination of the downside bet.
- **AD:** at 49% he is -0.67pp. At an observed 85% (just above his 83% break-even) his EV under
  the CURRENT branches is only **~+0.03pp**, i.e. a swing of order **+0.7pp**, not +1.5. He clears
  the bar but barely, because his healthy-branch on-court fit is itself only modestly positive
  (+0.30). NOTE: this holds the healthy-branch impact fixed. If observing AD healthy is also taken
  to RAISE that impact (a healthier AD plays better, not just more games), the swing is larger,
  but that is a separate, stated assumption, not what the break-even arithmetic alone gives.
- **Tatum / Haliburton:** opponents, but resolving their health sharpens the entire East side
  of the bracket MIN must navigate, and the BOS variant alone moves the title field by 10-20%
  of Boston's number.

In expectation, the right to **convert whichever of three or four fragile stars proves
healthy from a negative-EV gamble into a positive-EV move** is worth, conservatively, on the
order of **+0.5 to +1pp of title equity** per star resolved (Kyrie's swing is ~+0.9pp, AD's
~+0.7pp under current branches), and that is before counting the avoided cost of betting on the
one who does not recover. For a team whose entire title probability sits around 1.7%, even an
optioned ~+1pp is large: a meaningful fraction of the whole title number, available for the
price of patience rather than picks.

## Baseline aging: the decay premise does not survive contact with the model

Patience is a priced FALLBACK, not a free hold, and one consistent number now carries the point.
An earlier draft argued standing pat "does not bleed relative equity", citing a baseline-title
decomposition that read roughly flat-to-up under league-wide aging (~1.70% to 1.91%, a small
+0.09pp rival-decline tailwind). **That figure is RETIRED:** it was confounded by the rating
anchor (MIN's net is anchored to its measured 2025-26 net plus a roster-change delta, so aging
MIN's own incumbents largely cancels in the delta and cannot be cleanly split from rival aging).
The honest number comes from pricing the actual patience portfolio on the unified spine.

**Portfolio D (keep Randle, re-sign Dosunmu, no counterparty) prices at roughly -0.5pp of
2026-27 title** (four-view -0.6 to -0.2) with **-1.0 to -1.8pp of gauntlet erosion specifically
against OKC and SAS** -- the young gates that rise while MIN stands pat. So patience is not free:
it cedes about half a point now and loses ground to the rising young contenders. It is also not a
collapse: MIN's youth limits internal decay (a direct minutes-weighted ~-0.23 net, the young core
offsetting the Gobert/Conley age), and CBA-wise the patience year is not frozen, keeping Randle
and re-signing Dosunmu lands ~$210.5M (over the first apron), so MIN is down to the taxpayer
mini-MLE (~$6.1M, hard-capped at the second apron) plus minimums, enough for a cheap shooter, not
the full tool stack.

The point for the option argument is unchanged and now honestly priced: **patience costs ~half a
point of title now**, the premium paid to buy the 2027 information (the fragile stars' health) and
a clean cap sheet. That premium is small next to the +0.9pp Kyrie swing or the range a resolved
market opens, so the option still dominates a 2026 desperation-star gamble, but it is a real
premium, not a free hold.

## The internal-growth pillar, priced as cones (not a qualitative claim)

The earlier draft asserted "MIN's youth limits decay". That is now comp-based development cones
(25 years of historical comps, blocks-based rim signal, first-round-only for Beringer's set,
distributions not point estimates). **Selection-bias caveat:** minutes are endogenous (early run
selects for quality), so the runway branch is INFLATED and the buried branch OVERSTATED as causal
estimates; we partially de-bias (first-round pedigree, a groomed-backup branch, and splitting
"buried" into blocked-then-emerged vs failed-anywhere), and deflating the gap strengthens the
hold-Gobert verdict from both sides at once.

- **Beringer (turns 20 in November, wide cone, rim-anchor ladder).** The decisive branch under
  the recommendation is the GROOMED-backup path: a young first-round big given ~700-1500 backup
  minutes behind a starter lands at **46% rotation-rim-protector-or-better by 2027-28**, versus
  **65% on the (selection-inflated) starter-track runway**. The raw "blocked = 7%" earlier was an
  artifact of mixing failed-anywhere washouts into the buried group; talented-but-stuck comps did
  fine. **The point: keeping Gobert and grooming Beringer as the backup-five develops him nearly
  as well as trading does** (46% vs an inflated 65%), so the development cost of keeping is small.
- **Shannon (turns 26 in July, narrow plateau cone, bench-creator ladder).** A/C grant his runway
  automatically (Randle out plus DiVincenzo's absence open the bench-scoring role), so the runway
  branch is operative: **63% dependable-bench-scorer-or-better, ~26% 6MOY-tier-or-above**. Upside
  is minutes, not skill; a dependable role is a success on his ladder.

**A/C are an active development plan, not just a hold.** The recommendation now reads: keep Gobert,
make Beringer the designated backup-five (and add NO veteran big who blocks him), and hand Shannon
the vacated bench-creator minutes. Run at the cones' P25/P50/P75 the A/C title verdict is
cone-INDEPENDENT (the kids are depth behind Gobert, not load-bearing), so the verdict never rests
on the kids hitting, but the plan still captures their development internally, without paying the
both-out path's present-title cost.

## Why the bridge does not cost the option

The disaggregation path makes the bridge nearly free:

- **It raises the floor now.** The recommended Portfolio A/C (Randle to the Jalen Smith
  stretch-five + a TPE flier + an MLE shooter + the Dosunmu Bird re-sign) is +1.6 to +3.4
  risk-adjusted (DARKO-anchored), keeping MIN competitive this season without a fragile gamble.
- **It spends only modest future picks.** The asset lens prices the Randle dump sweetener at a
  target of seconds (a lightly-protected first only as the ceiling concession), so the 2028/2030
  firsts that underwrite this very option largely survive.
- **It keeps 2027 clean.** The pairs are built from short or expiring contracts, so the 2027
  cap sheet and pick stash stay intact, preserving the ability to strike when the coins land.
- **The value-extraction conversion (item 3a) is itself a patience asset.** Converting Randle
  into one cheaper shooter sheds salary to ~$178.8M; the on-court EV (+0.11) does not capture
  the RESIDUAL OPTION VALUE of that room beyond the used MLE: in-season deadline flexibility, tax
  and repeater relief, and a cleaner 2027 war chest. A near-break-even on-court move that also
  buys financial optionality is a coherent bridge step even though the title number is flat.

So the bridge is not a sacrifice of the contention window; it is the floor-raising move that
*funds the option*. Stay competitive, learn the health of the fragile stars on someone else's
dime, and in July 2027 make the star move from a position of information rather than a
gamble.

## The recommendation

1. **Execute the disaggregation now** (Avdija+Camara profile, or the durable-pair archetype):
   raises the floor, diversifies risk, keeps Gobert/Dosunmu/Conley, spends no 2027 capital.
2. **Hold the 2027 option open**: do not commit picks or cap to a 2026 fragile-star gamble
   (AD, Morant) that the math already rejects, and do not force the Kyrie bet before the knee
   is known.
3. **Re-run the board in July 2027** with the observed-health inputs; the fragile star who
   recovers becomes the upgrade, priced at a known availability rather than a coin flip.

## Flagged as a future build (not this memo)

A full **two-season Monte Carlo** would make the option value exact: simulate 2026-27 with the
bridge roster, draw each fragile star's health from its prior, then optimize the July-2027 move
conditional on the observed draw, and compare the distribution of two-year title equity to the
"swing now" path. That quantifies the option precisely. It is a substantial build and is
deliberately out of scope here; this memo establishes the argument and its rough magnitude
using the existing break-even framework.
