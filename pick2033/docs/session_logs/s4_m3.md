# Session 4 (M3) — Model C: aging curves
2026-07-01 (ran same day as S2; neither needed the spells review)

## Built
`src/models/aging.py`: delta-method BPM aging, natural cubic spline
(knots 21-36), hierarchical by archetype (guard/wing/big from listed
position), non-centered player random effects, Student-t residuals.
Survivor-bias mitigation per spec: dropout seasons (500+ mp then no
qualifying next season) imputed as decline-to-replacement (-2.0 BPM),
flagged. 14,496 delta rows (2,861 imputed), 2,432 players.

## v1 -> v1.1: imputed rows get their own residual scale
The single-scale v1 with-imputation fit (the authoritative one past 30)
hit R-hat 1.60: imputed rows are a different measurement process
(systematically large negative deltas) and destabilized nu/sigma. v1.1
gives them a dedicated sigma_imp. All three fits now converge
(R-hat 1.0050-1.0055, ESS 687+).
