# Bounding check: does LaMelo reviving Gobert's roll game change the verdict? (2026-06-29)

A fast, back-of-envelope UPPER BOUND, not a re-pricing. Question: is the LaMelo-Gobert pick-and-roll
synergy big enough to matter to the trade verdict, or is it real-but-immaterial? Held separate from the
main model; do not fold in unless this review says it is material.

## Verdict up front

Checked, it is immaterial to the headline. The only slice that is not already priced (the interaction
premium an additive model misses) is roughly +0.3 to +0.5 net rating at the generous end, which sits at
or inside the model's own noise floor (~+/-0.5 net) and well inside the metric-fork disagreement on the
whole trade (~0.8 net). It does not move "wash to slightly negative." It also fades in exactly the
setting that decides the title (playoff half-court). A gradeable watch-item is logged below.

## The data (Gobert offensive roll-man, synergy, RS)

| window | roll poss/game | points per roll (ppp) | pts/game |
|---|---|---|---|
| current 2025-26 | 1.52 | 1.270 (79th pct) | 1.93 |
| recent MIN 2022-25 | 2.26 | 1.235 | ~2.8 |
| Utah peak 2018-22 | 3.54 | 1.336 | ~4.7 |

The decline this year is a VOLUME story, not efficiency: his roll ppp (1.270) is already elite (higher
than every high-volume roll man in the 2025-26 league; the next best is Ayton at 1.235). What cratered is
how OFTEN he rolls (1.52/game, down from 2.26 recent and 3.54 at his peak). There is almost no efficiency
headroom; a revival is mostly "roll more often," not "finish better."

## The naive gross ceiling (this is NOT the answer; it double-counts)

Optimistic everything: restore volume to the Utah peak (3.54/game) AND lift efficiency to his peak
(1.336): 3.54 * 1.336 = 4.73 pts/game, vs 1.93 now, a gross +2.8 pts/game (~+2.8 net). This is the figure
to REJECT. Most of it is LaMelo's passing, which the model already prices in his full offensive rating,
plus a scheme choice (call more PnR). Counting it would double-count.

## The marginal premium (what an additive model actually misses)

The model already prices LaMelo's full offensive value, including his PnR creation, and Gobert's finishing
at his current/recent rating. So the restored roll VOLUME is already paid for: it is LaMelo doing what
LaMelo does (high-PnR creation, transported) plus Finch calling more of it. The genuinely new,
super-additive slice is narrow: LaMelo's lobs find an ELITE finisher (Gobert ~1.30) instead of the lesser
roll men his Charlotte rating was built on (~1.10), on the volume he restores.

- Finisher-quality premium on the incremental rolls: ~2.0 extra poss/game * (1.32 - 1.10) ~= +0.44 pts/game.
- Efficiency premium on his base rolls (1.27 -> ~1.34, and only partly LaMelo's doing): ~+0.05 pts/game.
- Interaction premium, generous upper bound: ~+0.5 net. Realistic: ~+0.3 net.

## Floor comparison

The title-delta floor is +/-0.5 to 0.8pp; translated through the sim that is ~+/-0.5 net. The whole trade's
net delta runs from -1.1 (Gueye-replacement, RAPM) to +0.83 (box), and the two metrics already disagree by
~0.8 net on the trade itself. A +0.3 to +0.5 net premium is at or inside that floor and smaller than the
fork disagreement the analysis could not resolve. It cannot move the verdict.

## Playoff caveat (where it is most vs least potent)

A revived roll game is a regular-season-friendly effect. MOST potent in the RS: transition, early offense,
scrambling and tired defenses, the lob threat in open space. LEAST potent in the playoffs: over a seven-game
half-court series, defenses tag the roller, sit in drop or pre-rotate, and Gobert (no pull-up or short-roll
playmaking counter) gets schemed off the action, the same series-context wall the defense lever hit, and
compounded by LaMelo's own availability and playoff-defense risk. So the slice that is already small is
smallest exactly where the title is decided. That deepens the immaterial read.

## Watch-item (gradeable, worth having either way)

Gobert's offensive roll-man VOLUME is the tell, not his efficiency (already elite). Log, first 20 games:
- Gobert offensive PRRollMan poss/game and points-per-roll (synergy), vs his 2025-26 baseline (1.52/game,
  1.270 ppp) and recent MIN baseline (2.26/game, 1.235 ppp).
- Revival signal: roll volume climbs back above ~2.5-3.0 poss/game with ppp holding >= ~1.27. That would say
  LaMelo is genuinely feeding the roll game.
- Null signal: volume stays near ~1.5/game. Then the revival did not happen and this bound is moot.
Grade date: align with the project's 2027-04-15 forward-prediction log.

## One-line summary

Even at an optimistic upper bound, the LaMelo-Gobert roll synergy the additive model misses is ~+0.3 to
+0.5 net, inside the noise floor and weakest in the playoffs, so it does not change the wash-to-slightly-
negative verdict; the gradeable tell is whether Gobert's roll VOLUME (not efficiency) climbs in the first 20
games.
