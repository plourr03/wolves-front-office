# The Bet

A four-part series for Wolves to a T on Minnesota's 2026 offseason, written first person and gated after: every number in the four parts is on `outputs/final_numbers.csv` or in the C1 to C4 tables, checked by `scripts/gate_prose.py`. The methods for the whole series (the four views, the two aging bases, the allocators and cells, the bands and labels, the corrections) live in one place: [the methods page](../methods.md).

| part | title | one line | hands off with |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What Connelly did | Opens at Kuminga's introduction on September 10, goes back to June 13 and walks the summer forward: the Dosunmu deal, the trade, the Saturday deadline, every dollar and pick in and out, the market's 3.16% and the grades, then back to the podium, where the summer ended with a player choosing Minnesota. | "One of the biggest things we found had nothing to do with the offseason at all, and everything to do with what a defense can do to Anthony Edwards." |
| 2 | What we found | Better or worse depends on Cody Williams's minutes and how you count DiVincenzo's Achilles; Kuminga and LaMelo are real; the Edwards finding is why Ball is here; most September arguments can't be measured; where the model and the market disagree. | "If the odds can't see what wins, what did the last eleven champions actually have?" |
| 3 | How champions actually happen | Eleven champions, one passage each, the Knicks at the center; on eleven seasons no single feature separates the champions from the 48 teams priced beside them; what every champion had was a top-six net rating and a top-three seed by April, health in the spring, and a top five that carries 70% of the minutes; Denver 2023 is the closest analog by price and by build. | "Whether Minnesota has eight guys like that is the question I'll try to answer in the last part. That one isn't the work. It's my case." |
| 4 | Why you should watch | Bobby's case: everything that could go wrong with its numbers, the Edwards clock the grades missed, the seven verdicts that survived, the dates, and why this is a season to be in the building for. | "See you at game 20." |

Files per part: `partN.md` (the article), `partN_short.md` (the feed version), `partN_quotes.md` (five pull-quotes with their sheet keys), `partN_slide.json` (the snapshot-slide config, four tiles keyed to the sheet).

The preseason title odds for all eleven champion seasons come from Basketball-Reference's preseason odds pages (courtesy sportsoddshistory.com), fetched, checked and written by `scripts/c1_preseason_odds.py`; Part 3's feature comparison still runs on the three most recent seasons.
