# The Bet, Part 2: What we found (feed version)

The first time I ran the season, the model said the summer made us worse. I ran it again, then went looking for why. The honest answer is a story: it depends on Cody Williams, the kid Utah threw into the Green dump, and on how you count an Achilles. The model's default gives Williams 16.1 minutes a night, which turns out to be an artifact of how it orders a roster; players like him who moved to a top-ten team kept 48% of their minutes, 11.7 for him, and below 12.6 the four ways we score players stop agreeing the summer hurt. DiVincenzo's tear costs 0.39 points of title odds on its own, and you can call that a decision or weather. Those are the same wound: a healthy Donte is the player who takes Williams's minutes.

Three things I'm sure about. Kuminga is a real upgrade on whoever else would play the four, 0.44 points of title odds, and it held under every test; the only thing that flips it is Beringer, on 7.9 minutes a game of rookie evidence. LaMelo in is the one clearly positive move, 0.79 points; Naz out is clearly negative, 0.33, and I haven't made my peace with that one. And Anthony Edwards, which starts on a couch in May: of the 150 highest-volume scorers he sits at the 1st percentile for what his primary defender takes off him, and over three seasons and 245 scorers he is the lowest of all of them, with 61% of his makes unassisted against a league median of 35%. New star pairings cost shots, not efficiency. That's why LaMelo is here.

The humbling part: most of what we argue about in September can't be measured. Zero of eight regular-season traits predicted 195 playoff series. "Defense travels" points the other way.

And the model was wrong in public once: Charlotte at 5.86% until I found the pipeline crediting baskets to the wrong team. Fixed, refit, Charlotte to 3.44%, Minnesota barely moved. The scoreboard now: 1.68% against the market's 3.16%. The biggest disagreement is Boston, 18.33% against 5.47%. If the odds can't see what wins, what did the last eleven champions have? Part 3.
