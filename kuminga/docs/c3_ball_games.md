## The base rate: what a season after a history like his looks like

Definition: player-seasons from 2001-02 to 2025-26, age 23 to 27, at least 24 minutes per appearance the season before, and at least 3 of the five prior seasons at 60% or less of the team's games (a season with no box score in the middle of a career counts as zero). Variant B also requires a healthy prior season, at least 80% of games, which is Ball's case (88% in 2025-26). C loosens the history to two of five prior seasons and D widens the age band to 22 to 29, both as sensitivity, because the strict cohort is small. Outcome: games played in the season that follows, on an 82-game basis. The band row is the same age and role band with no history condition.
