# The Bet, Part 4: Why you should watch (feed version)

This one is my case, so start with everything that could go wrong. LaMelo's games: 51, 75, 36, 22, 47, 72. Play him 50 instead of 82 and the title odds barely move, but the chance of a top-six seed drops 16.6 points. Cody Williams's minutes are an open question. Kuminga can opt out, and if he's good enough to, the most Minnesota can pay him without cap room is $7,276,800. The 2033 first is gone unprotected. They're $8,062,416 over the first apron, and Konchar's $2,055,000 a year lands through Anthony Edwards's walk year.

Now the argument the grades missed. Ant is signed through 2028-29 with no options. The supermax only opens if he makes All-NBA this season, which takes 65 games, and he played 61. On a .600 team the chance he leaves is 0.8% this season, 7.3% next, and 44% at the 2028-29 cliff; on a .450 team, 56%. Stars in a walk year on .600 teams left 31% of the time; under .500, 56%. Every C+ grades the summer against a safe alternative, and the clock says it doesn't exist.

The case: LaMelo is the clear win, 0.88 points of title odds. Kuminga is a real upgrade on the alternative, 0.51, and it held under every test.
