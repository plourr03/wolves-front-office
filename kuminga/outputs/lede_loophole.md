**Roster minimum:** CBA Article XXIX, Section 2(a) requires 14 or 15 players on the Active and Inactive Lists during the regular season; Section 2(b)(i) permits 12 or 13 for no more than two consecutive weeks at a time and 28 days in total.

**Hard cap:** the rule prohibits *exceeding* the apron, so a team salary exactly equal to it is legal. That is why the partial-exception figure lands on the dollar.
