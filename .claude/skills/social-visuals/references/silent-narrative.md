# Silent narrative (no voiceover, built for muted viewers)

These posts have no voiceover. The on-screen text is the narration, and most people watch with the sound off, so the text and the motion carry the entire story. Music sets mood, it does not carry meaning.

## The arc
Same shape we use everywhere: hook, build, turn, payoff, sign-off. The only difference is the words are read, not heard, so they are shorter and the motion does more of the work.

- Hook (first 1 to 2 seconds): a text line that creates tension, plus a visual jolt (the chart snapping in, one big number, a shape). The first second decides whether anyone stays. Example: "Everyone saw Ant's playoff dip. Few saw what changed."
- Build: reveal the baseline data. One short line names what we are looking at while the visual draws in.
- Turn: the surprise or the shift. The visual changes (a comparison flips, a circle draws, numbers move) and a text line lands the twist.
- Payoff: the consequence or takeaway, one line, the gut punch.
- Sign-off card: a clean end frame with the wordmark and "More at Wolves to a T." This replaces the spoken call to action.

## Rules
- One short text line at a time. A few words, large, high contrast, inside the safe area. If a viewer cannot finish reading it before the next beat, it is too long or too fast. Aim for 1.5 to 3 seconds per line.
- Something changes every couple of seconds. Silent plus static gets scrolled. A chart animating, a number counting, a highlight moving, a comparison shifting, any of that holds attention in place of a voice.
- Keep it tight. With no voiceover to pace it, shorter wins. Fifteen to thirty seconds is plenty, and tighter helps completion rate.
- Let the data set the beats. The numbers you validated decide what the turn and the payoff are. Do not script a punch the data does not support.

## Music
- Royalty-free or original audio is the safe default and will not hurt reach.
- Trending in-app audio can boost discovery, since a post on a hot sound gets surfaced to people browsing that sound. Use it when it fits the energy.
- Heads up: business or branded account types have a limited music library on Instagram, while creator and personal accounts get fuller access. Check which type the account is on.
- The post must work fully muted. Never lean on a beat drop to land a moment the visual does not already land on its own.
