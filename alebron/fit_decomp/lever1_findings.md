# Lever 1: Can Edwards play efficiently off-ball? Built 2026-06-26.

Estimand: Edwards's scoring efficiency OFF-ball (catch-and-shoot, spot-up) vs ON-ball (pull-up,
PnR ball-handler, iso); plus whether high-usage scorers generally retain off-ball efficiency.
Data: tracking catch-and-shoot, synergy by play type, and a pre-registered criteria-based comp.

## Measured: Edwards's off-ball efficiency is ELITE and at/above his on-ball (POSITIVE)

Catch-and-shoot (off-ball) eFG by season: 0.617, 0.618, 0.591, 0.633, 0.713 (2021-22..2025-26),
high and TRENDING UP, on real volume (~3 catch-and-shoot 3PA/game). Spot-up synergy: 70th, 88th,
96th percentile across the last three seasons (PPP 1.09 -> 1.23 -> 1.31). His off-ball scoring is
his MORE efficient mode; his on-ball (PnR ball-handler ~0.9-1.0 PPP, iso ~0.8-1.0) is good but more
variable. So the prerequisite, that he does not NEED the ball to be efficient, is clearly met.

## The comp anchor (pre-registered criteria, taken as it returned)

Criteria: usage >= 0.28 AND catch-and-shoot FGA >= 2/g, 2024-25. N=31 high-usage scorers. Their
catch-and-shoot eFG ranges WIDELY (0.30 to 0.67; mean 0.532, median 0.552): off-ball shooting is
NOT automatic for a high-usage player (Giannis 0.30, Wiseman 0.33, Anthony Davis 0.44 are poor;
Curry 0.645, Durant 0.669, SGA 0.653, Jokic 0.671 are elite). Edwards's 0.633 ranks ABOVE 27 of 31,
in the elite tier. So his off-ball efficiency is real and comp-anchored, not a fluke, and not a
property every star shares. (Bonus: LaMelo's own catch-and-shoot eFG is 0.552, median-ish, so the
secondary off-ball reps the offense may ask of HIM are also serviceable.)

## NECESSARY BUT NOT SUFFICIENT (the honest frame)

This is the first lever to return a clear, measured POSITIVE, and it should be credited: the most
common bear-case mechanism for the offense (Edwards is a ball-dominant scorer who needs the rock)
is refuted. He is an elite off-ball shooter. But off-ball COMPETENCE is necessary, not sufficient,
and "Edwards can play off-ball" does NOT become "the fit works":

1. CAN, not WILL/at what volume. The data proves he is EFFICIENT off-ball; it does not prove he
   will accept FEWER on-ball reps to make room for LaMelo. That is a role and willingness question
   (he is the alpha; he currently runs 23-33% of his possessions as the PnR ball-handler), and it
   is not settable from efficiency data. Partial unknown.
2. The unlock then RIDES on the other levers. If Edwards plays more off-ball, LaMelo becomes the
   primary creator, so the offense's ceiling now depends on LaMelo's availability (Lever 5, leaning
   negative) and his playoff defense (Lever 3, the real risk). Edwards shooting well off the catch
   does not fix either.
3. So Lever 1 removes ONE obstacle (the offense can work with Edwards off-ball); it does not
   redeem the trade.

## The three pre-registered reads

- Positive read (off-ball eFG at/above on-ball, comps hold): SUPPORTED, strongly. 0.633 eFG, 87th
  percentile among high-usage scorers, spot-up 88-96th percentile, off-ball is his best mode.
- Negative read (off-ball materially below on-ball): NOT supported.
- Can't-tell read: NOT triggered for the efficiency question (clearly callable positive). The one
  residual unknown is behavioral (will he embrace the volume of off-ball role), not measurable here.

## Sample caveats

Catch-and-shoot eFG is a season aggregate on solid volume; the comp set is one season (2024-25,
N=31). The "added a lead guard" event is proxied by the high-usage-scorer catch-and-shoot
distribution rather than a clean event study, which is hard to construct; flagged as directional.

## Early signal

Edwards's off-ball possession SHARE in the first 20 games (does he actually cede on-ball reps to
LaMelo), and whether his catch-and-shoot eFG holds at higher off-ball volume. The efficiency is
proven; the deployment is the open question.

## Identifiability verdict

CALLABLE POSITIVE: the prerequisite is strongly met and is the trade's first piece of measured
upside, the offensive-fit fear that Edwards cannot play off-ball is refuted. But it is necessary
not sufficient: it is a CAN not a WILL, and the decisive risks (defense, availability) are
untouched. Credit the positive; do not let it inflate into "the fit works."
